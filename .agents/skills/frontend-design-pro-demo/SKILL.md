# Frontend Design Pro Demo (11 Core Aesthetics)

| # | Aesthetic | Key Characteristics | Palette Guidance | Typography |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Swiss Minimalism** | Rigid grid, hyper-structured hierarchy, asymmetric balance | `#FFFFFF`, `#0A0A0A`, `#F4F4F5`, `#E4E4E7` | Helvetica, Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk |
| 2 | **Neumorphism** | Extruded shapes, dual light/dark soft shadows, tactile surfaces | `#E0E5EC`, `#F0F3F8`, `#D1D9E6` | Clean neutral sans-serif |

| 6 | **Aurora Mesh** | Flowing multi-stop mesh gradients, organic blurred light fields | Purple/Indigo/Cyan blending over dark backdrop | Minimal sans display |
| 7 | **Retro-Futurism / Cyberpunk** | Neon accents on deep obsidian, data telemetry, scan lines | `#0A0B0E`, `#00FFCC`, `#FF0055`, `#FFE600` | JetBrains Mono, Orbitron, Space Mono |
| 8 | **3D Hyperrealism** | Perspective depth, layered z-axis cards, directional lighting | Calibrated lighting, specular reflections | Sleek display sans |
| 9 | **Vibrant Maximalist** | High-energy block color, asymmetric grids, bold typography | Cobalt, Tangerine, Mint, Magenta | Extra-bold headline grotesk |
| 10 | **Dark OLED Luxury** | True black `#000000`, brushed champagne/brass gold, micro-hairlines | `#000000`, `#121212`, `#D4AF37`, `#F5E8C7` | Playfair Display, Cinzel, Bodoni |
| 11 | **Organic Biomorphic** | Earth tones, pebble curves, linen/paper grain, botanical pastels | `#F5F0E6`, `#C8DBC8`, `#D8C7B2`, `#8C4A1D` | Serif headlines + warm humanist body |
